# Project plan

Remaining work only. Remove implemented work rather than marking it complete. Each milestone delivers a visible result and is reviewed before the next begins.

| Milestone | Deliverable | Visible result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organize photos using their metadata | Extract capture dates, dimensions, camera details, and available GPS metadata. Respect image orientation and show missing fields honestly. Add date and camera filters, plus capture-date sorting. Keep location details private with the rest of the library. | Open a photo's details and narrow the library to photos taken on a particular day or camera. |
| Tag photos and find them again | Create tags, add or remove them on individual photos or a selection, and search tag names. Combine tag and date filters, with pagination and a clear way to reset the search. | Tag several photos and retrieve exactly the matching photos with a search such as “Rome AND Cat,” including after refreshing or signing in on another device. |
| Have AI tag new photos | Connect a configurable vision provider through a Go worker and SQS. Keep provider credentials on the server. Agree on the model, image/metadata sent to it, and a processing budget before enabling calls. Suggest subject and scene tags without inventing precise locations. Show queued, processing, completed, and failed states, with bounded retries and a manual retry action. Preserve manual corrections across retries. | Upload photos and watch searchable AI tags appear without waiting on the upload screen. Inspect failures and retry them from the app. |
| Review untagged photos and teach the library new tags | Add a needs-review view for photos with no useful AI tags or uncertain suggestions, distinct from processing failures. Let the owner correct tags and define new ones with descriptions. Offer an explicit background backfill over older photos, showing its estimated cost, progress, and results while preserving manual corrections. | Find a photo the AI could not classify, define the missing tag, and run a backfill that finds older matching photos without re-uploading them. |
| Compare repeats and reclaim space | Detect exact duplicates and suggest groups of similar compositions. Label the distinction and allow incorrect groups to be dismissed. Add side-by-side comparison with zoom and metadata so the owner can choose the keeper. Require explicit selection and confirmation to move unwanted shots to a recoverable trash view; permanently deleting them is a separate action. Show the storage actually reclaimed after permanent deletion. | Review a burst of similar shots, keep the best, restore a mistakenly trashed photo, and deliberately delete unwanted originals. No photo is deleted automatically. |
| Share selected photos publicly | Keep uploads private by default. Let the owner explicitly mark selected photos as public and return them to private. Serve public photos through shareable pages without requiring viewers to sign in, while keeping private photos and all upload/edit actions restricted to the owner. Exclude private metadata, including GPS, from public pages and image files. | Open a published photo's link while signed out and view it. Private photos remain inaccessible, and returning a photo to private disables public access through the app; previously downloaded copies cannot be recalled. |
| Retry failed uploads and stalled processing | Add upload and thumbnail-processing recovery without repeating successful work or creating duplicate photo records. Record a processing deadline and explicitly detect expired jobs so a crashed or timed-out worker does not leave a photo marked as processing forever. Preserve uploaded originals and report expired jobs as failed or stalled. Agree on deadline duration, expiry detection, automatic retry limits, delays, retryable errors, and manual retry controls before implementation. Improve the upload-to-gallery transition so pending photos do not appear as assumed-landscape tiles: choose between reading image orientation in the browser during upload or keeping photos in a progress list until their thumbnails are ready. | Recover from failed uploads or stalled thumbnail processing while keeping uploaded originals and successfully processed photos. |
| Upload and browse Fuji RAW files | Accept Fujifilm RAW (.RAF) uploads and preserve the original files. Decide whether to extract embedded JPEG previews or develop RAW images for thumbnails and viewing, including behavior when no usable preview is available. | Upload Fuji RAW photos, browse viewable previews, and download the unchanged RAW originals. |
